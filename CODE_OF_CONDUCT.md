# Code of Conduct

OpenNTX contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, respectful, and focused on building a reliable source-available systems project.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and precise language.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Keep security discussions responsible and private when needed.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, licensing, and architecture.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic arguments after maintainers close a decision.
- Encouraging malware use, credential theft, stealth, evasion, or bypass behavior.
- Misrepresenting OpenNTX compatibility or licensing.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants when behavior harms the project.

Report conduct issues privately to the project owner or maintainers.
